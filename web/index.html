        About

        How it's built

        Where do the train positions come from?

        From BART's published GTFS timetable. Each train's position is worked out in your browser from its scheduled departure and arrival times, moving at an even pace along the track between stations. They are scheduled positions, so real trains running late won't show as late.

        Where does the ridership data come from?

        BART's hourly origin-destination ridership reports and Lyft's Bay Wheels trip data, both public.

        How does "Ask TransitPulse" answer questions?

        It writes a read-only SQL query against the ridership warehouse, runs it, and shows you the answer together with the exact SQL it ran. Queries that would change data or scan too much are refused.

        Thanks for riding along. The map, the numbers and the analyst all come from BART's own published data.

        Data sources

        • Ridership: BART ridership reports. Data provided by BART.
